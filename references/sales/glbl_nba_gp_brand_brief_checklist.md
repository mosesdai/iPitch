## 结构化清单（忠实抽取）

**文档头**：NBA Global Partnerships Brand Brief → Prospect Questions

### 问题（按原文顺序 · 共 13 问）

1. **Deal scope** — What are the business units, brands, products, services, etc. that will be included in a deal?
2. **Goals** — What are your current marketing and business goals/objectives?
3. **Brand identity** — What is the brand identity? What key brand attributes and core values should we focus on? What is the brand value proposition or competitive advantage(s)?
4. **Target demographic** — Tell us about the profile(s) of your target demographic(s). (e.g. consumers versus business partners, age range, household income, interests, habits/behaviors, etc.)
5. **Messaging / positioning** — What is your primary brand messaging? How are you positioning the brand to consumers in the market?
6. **Media mix** — Where do you find success in your media mix? Where do you invest most, or not at all?
7. **Priority markets** — What are your priority markets? Are you only looking domestically or are international regions important as well?
8. **Calendar priority** — What are the priority periods during the calendar year (months/seasons)?
9. **Partnership outcome** — What is the most important outcome from an NBA partnership? (e.g. brand awareness, brand loyalty, customer acquisition, change in brand perception, etc.)
10. **Measurement / KPIs** — How do you measure marketing partnerships? What KPI’s/metrics would you include?
11. **Decision process** — When it comes to doing a sports sponsorship deal, how are decisions made? Who are the key stakeholders that will evaluate and have a say in an opportunity?
12. **Budget（敏感）** — (If appropriate) Can you provide details on available budget or investment range for this partnership?
13. **Prior sponsorships** — (For those who have other sponsorship deals) What types of sport sponsorship activations have worked best for you in the past? Are there certain things you stay away from?

### 文末注（原文）

> **Note:** All questions above are only suggestions. While all the information above is helpful, local teams should deem what is appropriate to ask based on culture, relationship, etc.

**章节计数**：1 个文档标题块 + 1 个 Prospect Questions 列表（13 问）+ 1 条使用注 = **3 个内容块**（问题项 13）。
