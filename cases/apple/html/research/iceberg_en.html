15. Long description of the industry context: Why “global sports digitalization” will be discounted in China


Global sports media is migrating from cable bundles to platform subscriptions. The first is **content menu discount**: the same Apple TV brand, the live broadcast and film and television combinations available in China are different from those in North America; directly stating that "available in 60 countries" means that Chinese users are already in the same experience, it is a classic global one and one does not mean China's in-depth operation. The second is **Path Dependency Discount**: The default path for users to watch football, chat about football, and create new products is stored in local super applications and social networks. Even if technology companies have the advantage of pre-installed devices, they may not have the habit of "turning on and watching football". The third path is **Supervision and Compliance Discount**: data, distribution, payment, and content review require service innovation to take a narrower legal channel. If the plan relies on gray zone gameplay, it will die in the first round of legal affairs.


At the same time, the mobile phone industry itself is in a stage where the replacement cycle is lengthening and high-end localization is in parallel. Policy impulses (such as trade-in measures) may amplify a single quarter, but they cannot be regarded as the only explanation for the structural reversal. The implications of the industry context for the proposal are very specific: the definition of success should fall on the retail drama that can be done in compliance with the service attachment, rather than the fantasy of rebuilding a set of North American copyright players in China.
