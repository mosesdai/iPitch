## 14. Finance narrative “dual lens”: sales recitation version

**Lens 1 · Group strong**: FY25 total net sales at the **$416.161B** order; Services crossed **$109.158B**; gross margin and operating income rose together. This is not a client “buying exposure to survive” — it is a platform company that already made Services a second curve globally. Talk board language: installed base, Services attachment, geographic-segment volatility — not media placement tables.

**Lens 2 · Greater China volatility**: Same fiscal year, Greater China net sales fell from FY24 **$66.952B** to **$64.377B**; looking further back, after FY22 peak **$74.200B** it has been stair-stepping down. Share fell from ~17% to ~15.5%. That is not a media-made “China story” — it is a structural fact on the 8-K tables. Then FY26 Q1 printed **$25.526B**, ~+38% YoY — classic misreads follow: some declare “China is gone”, others declare “full recovery”. Both wrong. Correct read is **dual timescales coexist**: annual still recorded pressure; quarter proves product pulse can flip the print.

**Lens 3 · Services smooth, hardware pulse**: Services revenue is relatively continuous across quarters; iPhone can extreme-amplify in Holiday. If China’s public narrative only celebrates the pulse, ordinary product quarters re-expose “cannot tell a story without units”. The financial reason for a sports/culture OS is reducing narrative variance dependence on a single hardware quarter — not “buying heat” for one ship quarter.

If sales can remember only three lines: (1) group Services is already $100B-class; (2) Greater China annual and quarter must be stated together; (3) sports hangs on Services products, not empty iPhone line items.
