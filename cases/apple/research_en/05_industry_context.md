## 15. Industry context long form: why “global sports digitalization” discounts in China

Global sports media migrates from cable bundles toward platform subscriptions. Apple used MLS and Friday Night Baseball to prove it can write league calendars into OS-grade apps; Disney, NBCU, and Amazon brought NBA U.S. national broadcasts into a new 11-year structure. On the surface one “tech swallows sports” wave — in China three discounts appear.

First, **content-menu discount**: same Apple TV brand offers different live/film mixes in China vs North America; saying “60 countries can watch” as if China users already share the same experience is classic global-101 ≠ China deep ops. Second, **path-dependence discount**: default watch/chat/second-create paths settle in local super-apps and social nets; even with device preload, tech may lack “open and watch” habit. Third, **regulation/compliance discount**: data, distribution, payments, content review force narrower legal lanes; gray-zone designs die in legal round one.

Meanwhile the phone industry itself sits in longer replacement cycles plus domestic premiumization; policy pulses (e.g. trade-in style measures) may amplify a quarter but cannot be the sole structural-reversal explanation. Industry context implication is concrete: success definition should land on compliant retail drama and Services attachment — not fantasy rebuilding a North American rights player in China.
