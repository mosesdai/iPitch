## Quote-bank long form

The quote bank is not a “golden line museum” — it counters two diseases: sales forging leader quotes, and upgrading call media notes into 8-K. Correct use: knife pulls at most one Newsroom/SEC-linked line proving the client already wrote Services or records into public narrative; deep-talk adds 2–3 by theme. If China-related is only call paraphrase, honestly mark C so counterpart sees you understand levels.

Re-asking beats reciting. Rather than recite Cook “above expectations”, ask: when you wrote that into the release, did China team’s “expectations” include a non-hardware pillar? Rather than praise Garber “redefine viewing”, ask: what is the China product sentence for redefine? Highest value of quotes is leverage, not oratory.

---

## Citation conflict handling

When Newsroom conflicts with call notes, prefer Newsroom and financial statements. When Chinese media translation exaggerates, return to English original. When partner (MLS, NBA) and Apple language disagree, present both originals — do not cherry-pick sides. Update cadence: within 72h after each earnings; within 24h when sports Newsroom changes product form.

## Welding quotes to tension

Welding formula: quote (Services or records) + table (Greater China dual timescale) + product fact (MLS fold-in). Missing any link, weld fails. Only dump 2.5B devices without Greater China table → global trivia; only Greater China soft without Services quote → doom talk; only MLS without China table → global slideshow. Quote bank serves welding, not flex.
