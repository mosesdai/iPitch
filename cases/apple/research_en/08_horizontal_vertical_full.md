## Horizontal×vertical long form: how to pull one knife from iceberg

Vertical answers “why now”: Services strategy mature, sports productization completed key jump, Greater China just lived annual pressure colliding with quarter pulse, installed-base topic re-lit. Horizontal answers “why this knife”: Amazon occupies U.S. rights table, Huawei squeezes premium uniqueness, ByteDance takes narrative half-life, Samsung reminds globally-strong/locally-weak failure mode. Cross-cut is not “so buy exposure” but “so need China culture-grade OS absorbable by retail & Services metrics”.

When selecting knife, delete all industry trivia and competitor trash-talk; keep one tension the counterpart cannot brush off with a single shipment quarter, plus one live evidence (finance or productization fact), plus one recognize-ask pointing at metric owner. The fuller this chapter, the fewer words on the knife — iceberg model math.
