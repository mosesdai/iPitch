## 14. Power & sales discipline long form

Cold-starting Apple, the common failure is not “creative not flashy enough” but **emailing the wrong inbox while thinking you’re talking decision**. Agencies can warmly arrange “leadership meetings” yet steal the agenda into placements & quotes; China marketing may listen to Store events yet lack final Services-metric ownership; Cupertino Services execs may reply interesting yet never appear on Greater China budget signature pages. MEDDIC here is not form-filling — it is anti self-deception.

Metrics must first clarify: does counterpart this quarter own Greater China net sales, store traffic, Services trials — or only exposure? Before EB is named, any price talk becomes noise and trains the client to treat you as a media vendor. Decision Criteria should force three spoken lines — e.g. brand-safety zero incidents, no stealing keynote, kill-switch in 6–8 weeks — more executable than empty “youthfulness”. Champion wants someone whose scorecard improves if you succeed — usually retail or marketing, not a personal basketball fan.

The last ask of the 10-minute script should point to metric owner and next-week sync — not “we’ll send a quote”. If all three layers only reply “email materials”, demand gate is closed — stay in nurture; do not self-soothe with discounts.

## 15. Champion portrait & anti-portrait

True Champion: actively wants metric definitions, willing to bring you to retail or finance, fears brand smash more than high quotes. Fake Champion: only pushes rights lists, stresses “leadership busy, quote first”, dumps everything to agency media, refuses kill-switch talk. Meet fake Champion: stay polite, downgrade materials to education pack, do not enter commercial terms. Cruel truth of power chapter: without Champion, the thickest iceberg is only your private library.

---

## 16. Dignified exit after failure

If within six weeks Champion and metric owner remain unidentified, write downgrade to industry-exchange materials and stop selling pilots. Dignified exit leaves professionalism — more likely to reopen next window than discount cling. Power chapter also manages the courage not to talk.
