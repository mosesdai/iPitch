## 7. “Org absorbability” test

Proposal must pass:

1. **Who sees results in weekly meeting?** (retail? marketing? Services?)
2. **Conflict with next launch window?**
3. **Completable with existing stores & apps, no gray zone?**
4. **Failure killable within a quarter without brand damage?**
5. **Reportable in Apple’s own words (Services, installed base, traffic)?**

Three+ “no” among five → downgrade to nurture materials; don’t push D.

## 14. Org & lifecycle long form

Apple org culture is full of paradox: extreme product control makes experience a religion globally, yet must do localization surgery under local statutes and channel realities; Services revenue proves it is more than hardware, yet Greater China public victory declarations still heavily depend on iPhone pulse. Understanding this paradox matters more than memorizing a VP name — names change; paradox structure is stable.

Lifecycle: global Services curve left “need to prove existence”, entered “expand product categories & subscription value”; MLS folding into Apple TV is a maturity move. Greater China hardware curve is mature-market offense/defense: stair-steps after peak do not deny the brand — they raise demand for differentiated narrative. What remains early is “China culture-grade Services/retail OS” — may be built internally or catalyzed by partners. Partner windows often appear in the few quarters when hardware pulse just lifted installed base and org can still discuss “how to attach”; once victory is fully attributed to product itself, culture budget becomes decorative and cuttable.

Thus org chapter constrains the knife: proposal must translate into Apple’s own words (Services, installed base, traffic, brand safety), pass retail theology and legal veto, and admit we are not teaching Apple to make phones. Fail those three and it is not “not quoted yet” — it is “org cannot absorb”.
