## 14. Partnership methodology: does it look like something Apple would sign?

Apple global sports deals show three observable traits. First, **duration**: decade-scale means legal, finance, product willingly share content-asset depreciation — not quarterly ad buys. Second, **productization**: Season Pass standalone → Apple TV subscription means success criteria migrate from “rights publicity” to “subscription value proof”. Third, **selectivity**: baseball as Friday doubleheaders, soccer as full-league bucket — not indiscriminate purchase of all North American majors — capital and brand-risk constrained choice, not “capability excuse”.

Invert the three as probes for any China proposal: multi-season length? Enters account/retail/Services metric systems? Restrained enough not to create brand loss of control? If answers are all “one-shot star poster”, proposal conflicts with proven typology — downgrade at iceberg stage rather than push on passion at the table.

Train the boundary muscle repeatedly: U.S. NBA media rights settled with Disney, NBCU, Amazon; Apple not on main-package announcement list. If partnership history “casually” stuffs broadcast rights into value prop, you escalate a winnable retail/culture fight into an unwinnable media-rights fight you have no right to fight. Fatal especially on cold start — agencies love turning everything into rights rate cards.

---

## 15. Why China “non-sports” partnerships must not be ignored

Retail education events, developer conferences China sessions, enterprise/education procurement, carrier contracts — these are Apple’s real China operating muscle. Sports proposals that ignore them get treated as “out-of-town consultants” by local teams. Correct posture: first acknowledge muscle in retail & channel, then ask whether culture tendons can grow on that muscle. Partnership history must run dual tracks: global sports product line + China retail ops line. Only after reading both are you entitled to say “operating system”.

---

## 16. Meeting use of break points

Break points are not to humiliate the client for contradictions — they invite choosing a future identity: keep being a hardware-cycle narrator, or become a culture-product operator consistent with global Services identity. Tone is ask-to-recognize direction, not courtroom cross-exam. History provides evidence; sales provides respect.
