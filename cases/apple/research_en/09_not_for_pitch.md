## X. Don’t-say list long form: restraint is also combat power

The deeper you research, the easier to flex in meeting. Flex cost: drag meeting onto unwinnable battlefields — exact Apple tax rates, channel scandal details, nationalist opposition, U.S. broadcast rights, fake-precise shares, CNY conversion theater. These are not “unknown” — they are “known yet choose not to say” — exactly why chapter 09 exists.

Underwater insights arm ifalsify and deep-talk, but must not pollute first face. Example: we know Q4 Greater China still soft, so we won’t simplify to “missing a Spring Festival campaign”; we know Wearables annual sales down, so we won’t casually call Watch sports plans risk-free mainline; we know agencies love selling exposure, so we rewrite metrics on paper. Restraint is not cowardice — it keeps ammo for topics the counterpart opens. If they ask sensitive items, answer with traceability levels and return to demand & metrics — don’t ride the digression.
